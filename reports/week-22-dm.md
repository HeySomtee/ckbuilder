# Week 22 DM draft for Neon

Hey Neon, I've been building around your advice to make the testnet usable and give people a reason to come back. Streak now has its own domain: https://streakpitch.com

The biggest addition is Streak Five: a free weekly fantasy game with five EPL players, a budget and a double-points captain. It follows the official gameweeks automatically, with player form, prices, history and upcoming fixtures. I experimented quite a bit with the layout and selection flow so it feels like a game, with meaningful choices, rather than another dashboard. The weekly reset also means new users aren't months behind everyone else.

I also built Survivor: pick a winning team each round, but you can't use that team again. The interesting part is deciding when to spend your strongest teams.

For incentives, Five has gameweek sponsorship auctions. Sponsors can top up their bids; the winner funds a fixed reward pool and gets optional brand/product visibility, while losing bids are refunded. That gives supporters a role without promising rewards that could grow beyond the available pool. Everything is testnet tCKB for now, and I've completed a real sponsorship payment.

I've also been fixing wallet reconnection, slow Activity reads and confusing setup flows. Both games are beta, and I'm finishing the hosted checks before inviting people through the full journey. The aim is exactly what you suggested: make it fun to participate, then let actual user feedback shape what I improve next.
